Bourbon inventory app for iPhone

The bourbon app I built to keep track of my own shelf

In brief

Pour Picks is an iPhone app for tracking a bourbon collection. Take a photo of the front label and it identifies the bottle and adds it to your shelf with a price. The free version gives you 5 scans and 5 owned bottles. Cask Club is $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year and lifts both limits.

How much does Pour Picks cost?

Pour Picks is free to download, and the free version doesn't expire. Cask Club is the paid tier: $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, billed and renewed through the App Store. The yearly plan comes to about $2.08 a month, so if you searched for a whiskey inventory app under $5 a month, either plan fits.

Cask Club adds unlimited scans and Precision Match. It also opens the Wishlist and Finished shelves and takes the cap off Owned, and that's the whole list. Reviews and pairings are free, and so are Bourbon DNA and the Concierge.

Can I scan a barcode to add a bottle?

No. Pour Picks has no barcode scanner. You point your iPhone at the front label and take a photo. The app reads the label and matches it to a bottle in the catalog, then it goes on your shelf. Free accounts get 5 scans in total and Cask Club scans are unlimited, and you never have to turn a bottle around to hunt for a barcode.

If a barcode scanner is the one thing you need, OnlyDrams has one. Its listing says it shows you what you should be paying while you're standing in the store.

What does Pour Picks keep track of?

Bottles go on shelves. Everyone gets the Owned shelf, which holds 5 bottles on the free version and as many as you like on Cask Club. Cask Club also adds Wishlist (the bottles you're hunting) and Finished (the ones you've emptied).

Every bottle in your cellar has a price. It starts at the catalog's list price, and you can type in what you actually paid. If you own two of something it counts twice, and the total sits at the top of the cellar. The app doesn't pull auction or secondary-market prices. For that, Bourboneur and OnlyDrams both do a lot more.

Reviews are free. Each one has notes on the nose, palate and finish plus an overall rating. It stays with the bottle so you can find it the next time you pour.

Can it help me find a bourbon I'll like?

There are three ways in. Bourbon DNA gives you a palate profile. The Bourbon Concierge takes one bottle you love and returns its 10 closest matches. Both are free. Precision Match is part of Cask Club: a 10-question quiz that gives you your top 10 bottles with a reason next to each one.

You can also browse by distillery or by char level, and every bottle page lists food pairings.

Each of the other three does something better. OnlyDrams has by far the most ratings and runs on Android, and its barcode scanner and live pricing are made for checking a bottle in a store. BarrelBook costs a one-time $7.99 and works on iPad with iCloud sync. It also tracks infinity bottles. Bourboneur is built around what bottles sell for on the secondary market.

Pour Picks is the one built around the label photo and around matching: Bourbon DNA, the Concierge and Precision Match all point you at bottles you haven't tried yet.

Questions people ask before installing

Is there a free app to track a bourbon collection?

Pour Picks is free to download and the free version has no time limit. It includes 5 label scans in total and up to 5 bottles on the Owned shelf, plus reviews, food pairings, Bourbon DNA and the Bourbon Concierge. BarrelBook also has a free tier, which its listing caps at 5 bottles and 2 tastings.

Does Pour Picks scan barcodes?

No. You take a photo of the front label and the app identifies the bottle from that. OnlyDrams is the one to look at if a barcode scanner is a must.

Is there a whiskey inventory app under $5 a month?

Yes. Cask Club, the paid tier of Pour Picks, is $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year. Both are billed through the App Store, and the yearly plan works out to about $2.08 a month.

What do I get with Cask Club?

Unlimited label scans and Precision Match, a 10-question quiz that gives you your top 10 bottles with a reason for each. Cask Club also opens the Wishlist and Finished shelves and removes the 5-bottle cap on Owned. Everything else in the app is in the free version.

Can Pour Picks tell me what my bourbon collection is worth?

It keeps a running total at the top of your cellar. Each bottle starts at its catalog list price, and you can swap in what you paid. It does not track auction or secondary-market prices.

Does Pour Picks work on Android or iPad?

Pour Picks is an iPhone app. There is no Android version, and the App Store does not list it for iPad.

How do I cancel Cask Club?

Cask Club is an App Store subscription, so you cancel it on your iPhone in Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. It stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
