What Hibiki 21 Year Old tastes like

Hibiki bottles this as 21 Year Old out of Japan in the japanese category at 86 proof , aged 21 years. Its dominant notes are sherry, dried fruit, oak, toffee, chocolate.

  • Body: 4/5
  • Sweetness: 3/5
  • Char: 2/5
  • Proof: 86

Two decades of maturation deliver deep complexity with sherry influence, dried stone fruit, and polished oak. Smooth, sophisticated finish.

How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With sherry leading and a body of 4/5, Hibiki 21 Year Old can stand up to rich, fatty dishes without being flattened.
