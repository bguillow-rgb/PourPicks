What Glenmorangie 25 Year Old tastes like

Glenmorangie bottles this as 25 Year Old out of highlands in the scotch single malt category at 86 proof , aged 25 years. Its dominant notes are honey, dried fruit, toffee, vanilla, orange peel.

How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With honey leading and a body of 4/5, Glenmorangie 25 Year Old can stand up to rich, fatty dishes without being flattened. The sweetness means dessert works better here than it does with drier pours.
