How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With honey leading and a body of 3/5, Hakushu 18 Year Old is easily overwhelmed, so keep the plate lighter than you think.
