How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With caramel leading and a body of 4/5, Maker's Mark Cellar Aged 2024 can stand up to rich, fatty dishes without being flattened. The sweetness means dessert works better here than it does with drier pours. Heavy char rewards anything grilled or smoked.
