How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With tobacco leading and a body of 4/5, Demerara Diamond El Dorado can stand up to rich, fatty dishes without being flattened. The sweetness means dessert works better here than it does with drier pours.
