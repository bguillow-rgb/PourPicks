What Highland Park 50 Year Old tastes like

How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With dried fruit leading and a body of 5/5, Highland Park 50 Year Old can stand up to rich, fatty dishes without being flattened. The sweetness means dessert works better here than it does with drier pours.
