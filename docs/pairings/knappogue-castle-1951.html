What Knappogue Castle 1951 tastes like

Knappogue Castle bottles this as 1951 in the irish category at 80 proof . Its dominant notes are sherry, dried fruit, oak, honey, nuts.

An extremely rare vintage release, one of the oldest Irish whiskeys ever bottled, representing a historic piece of Irish whiskey heritage.

How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With sherry leading and a body of 3/5, Knappogue Castle 1951 is easily overwhelmed, so keep the plate lighter than you think.
