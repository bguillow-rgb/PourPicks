What Lagavulin 16 Year Old tastes like

Lagavulin bottles this as 16 Year Old out of Islay in the scotch single malt category at 86 proof , aged 16 years. Its dominant notes are peat smoke, seaweed, dried fruit, oak, brine.

Complex flagship: heavy peat, seaweed, and brine balanced with dried fruit and oak sweetness. Long, smoky finish.

How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With peat smoke leading and a body of 4/5, Lagavulin 16 Year Old can stand up to rich, fatty dishes without being flattened. Heavy char rewards anything grilled or smoked.
