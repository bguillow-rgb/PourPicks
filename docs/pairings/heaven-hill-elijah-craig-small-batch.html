How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With caramel leading and a body of 4/5, Heaven Hill Elijah Craig Small Batch can stand up to rich, fatty dishes without being flattened.
