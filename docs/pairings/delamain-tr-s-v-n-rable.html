How to think about pairing this bottle

The rule that holds across these matches is echo, not contrast: pick a dish that repeats one of the bottle's own notes. With dried fruit leading and a body of 3/5, Delamain Très Vénérable is easily overwhelmed, so keep the plate lighter than you think.
