Common questions

How do I delete my Pour Picks account?

Open the app, go to the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete Account and confirm. That permanently deletes your cellar, reviews, scan history, ratings and bottle suggestions, and it can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, email support@pourpicks.app from the address on your account with the subject "Delete My Account" and I'll remove your data within 7 days.

How do I cancel my Cask Club subscription?

Apple manages the subscription. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, then tap Pour Picks Pro (Cask Club) and Cancel Subscription. It stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting your account in the app doesn't cancel an active subscription, so you have to cancel it in iOS Settings too.

The scanner can't identify my bottle. What can I try?

Fill the frame with the front label and shoot in even, bright light without glare. Hold the phone steady so the text stays sharp. If it still misses, tap "Describe in words" to try again without the photo, search the catalog by hand, or save the bottle as your own. Right after you add a bottle there's a 4.5-second undo if you need it.

How old do I have to be to use Pour Picks?

You have to be of legal drinking age, which is 21 in the United States. The app checks your age the first time you open it.

Is Pour Picks a retailer?

No. Pour Picks doesn't sell or ship alcohol. It's for cataloging a collection and keeping track of what it's worth.

How many free scans do I get?

Everyone gets 5 free scans, signed in or not. Cask Club removes that limit, and every device or account has a cap of about 30 scans an hour and 100 a day.
