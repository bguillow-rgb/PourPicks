Features

What it does

Pour Picks is an iPhone app for bourbon collectors. It files the bottles you own, keeps a running value of the cellar, and lets you rate every pour.

Scan it into your cellar. The AI reads the label, including store picks and bottles with no barcode.

Bourbon DNA. A short quiz learns whether you lean sweet or spicy, easy sipper or cask strength. Then it names the bottle to buy next and why.

Reviews. Rate the nose, palate and finish on anything you pour. Reviews are free for everyone.

The whole American shelf. Browse American whiskey by distillery, age, proof or price.

Cask Club

What's in Cask Club?

Pour Picks is free to download. The free version holds 5 owned bottles and 5 label scans. Cask Club is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year, and it removes both limits.

It also adds Wishlist and Finished shelves and Precision Match: 10 questions, your top 10 bottles, and why each one fits.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Pour Picks?

It's an iPhone app I built to keep track of my own bourbon collection. You scan bottles into your cellar and rate what you pour, and a short quiz learns your palate.

Is Pour Picks a retailer?

No. Pour Picks doesn't sell or ship alcohol. It's for adults who want to keep track of what they own and what they've tasted.

How old do I have to be to use Pour Picks?

You have to be of legal drinking age, which is 21 in the United States. The app checks your age the first time you open it.

How does the bottle scanner work?

Point the camera at the front label and the AI reads the distillery, expression and proof, then finds the bottle in the catalog. You can fix anything it got wrong before it goes in your cellar. It works on store picks and bottles with no barcode, and if a bottle isn't in the catalog you can save it as your own.

Is Pour Picks free?

Yes. The free version holds 5 owned bottles and 5 label scans, and reviews are free for everyone. Cask Club is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year and removes both limits. It's billed through Apple, and you can cancel any time in iOS Settings.

Is my collection data private?

Yes. Your cellar, reviews, scan history and ratings are tied to your account. I don't sell them or use them for advertising. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Will Pour Picks be available on Android?

Not yet. Pour Picks is iPhone only for now.
