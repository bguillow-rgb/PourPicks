Press Kit

Short answer

Pour Picks is an iPhone app for bourbon collectors that I built on my own. It launched on the App Store on May 9, 2026. Brand assets and screenshots are available on request at support@pourpicks.app.

Pour Picks is an iPhone app for bourbon collectors. Scan a label and the AI adds the bottle to your cellar, then the app keeps track of what you own, what you've poured and what it's worth.

Why I built it

I kept my collection in a spreadsheet until it got too big to be useful. The question I asked most nights was what to pour from what I already owned, and the spreadsheet couldn't answer it.

So the cellar is the center of the app. Pour Tonight works from your shelf, and Bourbon DNA learns your palate before it suggests anything new.

Long · ~110 words

Pour Picks is an iPhone app for adult bourbon collectors, launched on the App Store in May 2026 by solo developer Bob Guillow. It reads a bottle's label with Claude Vision and adds it to a private cellar, and users can correct the match before it's saved. Bourbon DNA, a short palate quiz, names the bottle to buy next with the reasons it fits. Pour Tonight picks a bottle to open from the user's own shelf, weighted to their palate. The catalog covers American whiskey and grows through scan corrections. Pour Picks is bootstrapped, sells no data, shows no ads and does not sell alcohol. The app is free to download, and Cask Club is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year.
