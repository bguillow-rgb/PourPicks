Pour Picks

Inside the app

Six parts of the app, in about the order you'd use them, from your first scan to what your shelf is worth.

  1. 01 Cellar Owned · Wishlist · Finished

    Your cellar

    Every bottle is filed by distillery, expression, proof, age and fill level. The free version holds 5 owned bottles. Cask Club removes that limit and adds Wishlist and Finished shelves for what you're hunting and what you've emptied. When you can't decide what to open, Pour Tonight picks a bottle from your cellar, weighted to your palate.

  2. 02 Catalog Bourbon · Rye · Tennessee · Wheat · Malt

    The whole American shelf

    Browse American whiskey by distillery, age, proof or price, or open it up to world spirits. Bottles list the mash bill and flavor tags, and you can add one to your cellar from there. Every bottle page lists food pairings, free for everyone. I publish some of the pairing data on the site as bottle pairing guides.

  3. 03 Label scan

    Scan the label

    Works on store picks and bottles with no barcode.

    Point your camera at the front label and the AI reads the distillery, expression and proof, then finds the bottle in the catalog. You can fix anything it got wrong before it goes in your cellar. If the bottle isn't in the catalog, save it as your own. The free version includes 5 scans, and every add has a 4.5-second undo.

  4. 04 Palate Bourbon DNA · Concierge · Precision Match

    What to buy next

    Bourbon DNA is a short quiz that learns whether you lean sweet or spicy, easy sipper or cask strength. Then it names the bottle to buy next and gives you the reasons. Bourbon Concierge starts from a bottle you already love and finds the closest matches. Cask Club adds Precision Match, which asks 10 questions and gives you your top 10 bottles and why each one fits.

  5. 05 Reviews Nose · Palate · Finish

    Rate every pour

    Give each pour an overall rating plus separate marks for nose, palate and finish. You can note how you took it (neat, rocks, water, cocktail), the pour size, and anything else in your own words. Reviews are free for everyone, and your rating counts toward the average other collectors see on that bottle.

  6. 06 Value MSRP, or what you paid

    What the shelf is worth

    Your cellar total updates with every bottle you add. Each bottle starts at MSRP, and you can change it to what you paid. If you own more than one of something, you can set the count and the total includes each one.

Something off?

If something in the app looks wrong, email support@pourpicks.app and I'll take a look.
